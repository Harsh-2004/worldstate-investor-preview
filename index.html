01 / THESIS

The verification gap

A successful call is not a correct outcome.

As agents gain permission to issue refunds, move balances, and reconcile ledgers, traces become necessary—but insufficient.

Retries can duplicate value. Webhooks can arrive out of order. A provider may succeed after the agent times out. Business state may disagree with economic state.

03 / TRACE TO ECONOMIC TRUTH

A synthetic failure, step by step

Follow the money
as the story changes.

This is a presentation view of a controlled synthetic replay, not a live provider connection or customer incident. Its sequence is simplified for explanation; the evidence panel links to the bounded benchmark summary.

01 / THE REQUEST

One authorized refund.

The controlled fixture authorizes a $10 refund.

02 / THE AMBIGUITY

It times out after the effect.

The response is unknown. The provider has already started the economic operation.

03 / THE RETRY

The retained key is lost.

The synthetic mutation evicts the idempotency result, so a retry can create a second refund.

04 / THE SETTLEMENT

Two refunds settle.

The authoritative state now contains two successful $10 effects against $10 authorization.

05 / THE VERDICT

The invariant breaks.

WorldState checks provider state, not the agent’s success report, and records the reproducible failure.
